### Pipeline per block

header + pool logs → state of changed pools (Multicall3, tick windows cached) → USD prices → scan every
pool pair across DEXs → simulate flash-loan round trip over a size grid (exact V3 tick-crossing math,
verified to the wei against Uniswap, PancakeSwap and Slipstream quoters) → deterministic risk rules →
paper trade, resolved on the next block → features, swaps, snapshots, actions in Postgres.

### Pools

47 bootstrap pools over 13 pairs (WETH/USDC incl. 1 bp venues, cbBTC/USDC, cbBTC/WETH, cbETH/WETH,
wstETH/WETH, WETH/USDT, WETH/USDbC, USDC/USDbC, USDC/USDT, DAI/USDC, AERO, VIRTUAL). Discovery keeps
TVL/fees fresh and adds pools of allowlisted tokens above $200k TVL (347 known, ~60 active).

## Known limitations

- "Actual result" ignores the race to the sequencer; the lifetime tracker records who closed each spread.
- Quotes leaving the cached tick window are flagged inexact and blocked (cap 64 ticks, 160 on 1 bp pools).
- Aerodrome stable pools (different curve) are skipped; Uniswap V2 is allowlist-only (3M spam pairs).
- Reorgs are detected and flagged, not unwound.
