## 5. Leveraged yield loop (owner's shared post, analysed 2026-10-28)

The image the owner shared is a social post describing a carry loop: buy BTC → borrow USDC against it → put the USDC
into "long-term reliable pools" → use the pool income to buy more BTC → borrow again → compound. It is not arbitrage;
it is a leveraged long BTC position with LP carry on top. Priced with live numbers:

**Aave V3 Base** (read from the Pool contract, block ~53171000):

| asset | max LTV | liquidation threshold | liquidation bonus | borrow APR |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| cbBTC (collateral) | 73% | 78% | 7.5% | 0.65% |
| USDC (borrow) | 75% | 78% | 5.0% | 4.69% |
| WETH | 80% | 83% | 5.0% | 2.01% |

**Pool fee income**, 24 h of decoded swaps from the runner (`swaps` table), divided by anchored depth; full-pool
averages, a concentrated position earns a multiple while in range and nothing out of range:

| pool | volume/day | fees/day | fee APR on depth |
|---|---|---|---|
| univ3_usdc_cbbtc_5 | $6.5M | $3,227 | ~12% |
| aerocl_usdc_cbbtc_100 | $2.1M | $946 | ~19% + AERO emissions |
| univ3_weth_usdc_30 | $45.5M | $136,500 | ~26% |
| univ3_weth_cbbtc_5 | $16.9M | $8,453 | ~12% |
| univ3_weth_usdc_5 | $9.0M | $4,512 | ~13% |

Web aggregators quote the Aerodrome cbBTC/USDC pool anywhere from 33% to 200% APY (mostly emissions) and disagree too
much to use; the on-chain fee numbers above are the floor.

**Economics.** Carry = pool yield − 4.69% borrow ≈ 7–14 points on the borrowed USDC before impermanent loss.
Leverage from recycling: 1/(1 − LTV used) → 1.6× BTC exposure at ~37% LTV, 3.7× at the 73% maximum. Liquidation price:
collateral value = debt / 0.78, i.e. a 36% cbBTC drop at 50% LTV, a 6.4% drop at the maximum, then a 7.5% penalty.
A cbBTC/USDC LP is half BTC, so the pool leg partly offsets the collateral leg, and a concentrated range is short
volatility: a sharp move loses on both at once. It needs the owner's own capital held for months, not a flash loan.

**Verdict.** Opposite in character to the MEV runner (directional, capital-based, slow). One cycle at ≤ 40–50% LTV into
a deep cbBTC/USDC pool is defensible today (~10 points of carry on the borrowed part, liquidation only on a >36% crash);
the "compound, compound" recycling in the post is what gets people liquidated.

**Decision (owner, 2026-10-28): pursue leveraged yield as a second track alongside the arbitrage runner.** Next steps:

1. Compare venues before opening anything: Aave V3 Base (above) vs Morpho Blue cbBTC/USDC markets (often higher LLTV
   and a lower rate; the stack already talks to both as flash lenders). Record LLTV, rate, oracle and liquidity.
2. Fix the rules up front: max LTV (proposed 40%), one cycle only, pool and range (univ3_usdc_cbbtc_5 or
   aerocl_usdc_cbbtc_100), rebalance rule when price leaves the range, exit rule when carry < 0 for N days.
3. Build the monitor from the existing stack (new module, e.g. `mev/yield/`): health factor, distance to liquidation,
   realised fee APR of the position vs live borrow APR, in-range flag, AERO emissions if Aerodrome. Alert via the
   agent's webhook. Read-only; no automated borrowing or repaying in V1.
4. Size and fund the position (owner's decision, outside the repo). The runner's signer key is NOT the wallet for this.
5. Treat the position's P&L separately from the runner's `executions` table.
