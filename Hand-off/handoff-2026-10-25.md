## What changed today

### 1. The six paper trades were phantom (fee bug, fixed)

Replaying paper trade 4 (block 53038943, $1,000 WETH → VIRTUAL on Aerodrome → WETH on Uniswap V3 5 bp) on a
Base fork lost 1966879470454673 wei (~$4.58) instead of earning $1.68. The Aerodrome VIRTUAL/WETH pool
transferred exactly 1% of the input to its fee recipient; the registry had it at 0.3%. Checked every active
Aerodrome volatile pool against the factory (`getFee(pool, false)`): AERO/WETH and VIRTUAL/WETH charge 100 bp,
the other eight 30 bp. Those two pools are behind every "profitable" candidate in the dataset (25,570
VIRTUAL/WETH and 15,750 AERO/WETH candidates, all six risk passes, all 15,000 "blocked only by staleness").

With the right fee the Python simulator matches the chain to the wei (buy leg within 3510 wei of 1.6e21 —
Aerodrome deducts the fee before x*y=k, now modelled; sell leg within 1 wei). Fixes:
`mev/chain/addresses.py` (both pools 10000 ppm), `mev/db/session.py` (startup seeding no longer overwrites a
fee discovery refreshed — the API pod's restart had reset it), `mev/dex/constant_product.py` (Aerodrome rounding).

Consequence: **no real opportunity has been seen yet.** Non-Aerodrome candidates (WETH/USDC 1 bp venues,
cbETH/WETH, cbBTC) were all negative at the best size. The paper P&L of $8.86 is void.

### 2. Loop lag (fixed in code, not yet deployed)

The loop ran 5-7 blocks behind head: 4-6 sequential RPC round trips per block at 250-400 ms each (Mumbai →
Alchemy) against a 2 s block, then it worked through the backlog one stale block at a time. The cluster is
idle (loop 19 millicores), so this is latency, not compute. Changes in `mev/main.py` / `mev/config.py`:
`chase_head` (jump to the newest block when behind, count the skipped ones), the head is read concurrently
with the pool-state Multicall, the round-robin tick refresh pauses while behind, `max_state_age_blocks` 1 → 2.
Not done yet: WebSocket `newHeads` subscription (removes the poll), a US-region runner.
